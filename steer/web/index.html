steer

A writing console where the knobs are made from your own text, and every knob is checked.

  1. Press space or click write. The model adds the next sentences to the middle of the screen.
  2. Left numbers are how hard each line pulls on the next one. Long bar = the story keeps coming back to it.
  3. Right dials are mined from the passage: two ends of an axis and how hard to push. Drag one, then write again.
  4. Bottom left reads the new sentences back and says which dial each one actually landed on. unclaimed means the dial did nothing.

To force a word into the next paragraph: select it in the prose. It becomes an attention box.

The idea comes from Spaceship IDE by Chuanqi Sun. Everything measurable here — the audit, the blind test, the mined axes — is this project's addition.

blind test

The passage you have was continued twice from the same words. One of the two was written with a dial turned up. Pick the one the console was steering — then see what the auditor measured. Click a card, or press L / R.
